# Privacy Policy for CamSketch

**Last Updated:** January 6, 2026

M. Bilal Ahmed operates the CamSketch mobile application (the “App”). This Privacy Policy informs you of our policies regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of personal data when you use our App.

By using the App, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

## 1.	Information Collection and Use

CamSektch is designed with you privacy in mind. We collect minimal data to provide
and improve the service.

### a.	Data you Provide Directly:
* **Sketches & Images:** The drawings and images you create and choose to save within the App’s local storage.

### b.	Data Collected Automatically (Technical Data):	
* **Camera Feed (Core Functionality):** The App requires access to your device’s camera to provide the Augmented Reality sketching feature. **This live camera feed is processed in real-time on your device to generate the overlay and is not recorded, stored, or transmitted to our servers.**
* **Device Information:** We may collect non-personal information such as your device type, operating system version, and unique device identifiers (e.g., for crash reporting).
*  **Usage Data:** We may collect information on how the App is accessed and used (“Log Data”). This may include features you use, session length, and crash reports to help us improve stability and performance.

## 2.	How We Use Your Information

We use the collected data for the following purposes:
* To provide and maintain the core AR sketching functionality of the App.
* To detect, prevent, and address technical issues and crashes.
* To improve the user experience and develop new features.
* To communicate with you (if you have provided contact information).

## 3.	Legal Basis for Processing (GDPR)

If you are located in the European Economic Area (EEA) or United Kingdom (UK), 
our legal basis for processing your minimal technical data is our **legitimate interest** in operating and improving the App. The processing of your camera data is based on your **explicit consent** when you grant camera permission to the App.


## 4.	Data Sharing and Disclosure

We do **not** sell, trade, or rent your personal data.

We may share generic aggregated demographic information (not linked to any personal identity) 
for analytics purposes. We may employ trusted third-party service providers to assist us, such as:
*  **Google Firebase Analytics/Crashlytics:** To analyze app usage and fix errors. You can review
*  Google’s privacy practices [here](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

These third parties are obligated not to disclose or use your information for any other purpose.


## 5.	Data Retention and Deletion

* **Camera Feed:** Not retained. Processed in real-time and immediately discarded.
* **Sketches:** Stored locally on your device. You can delete them at any time through
  the App’s interface. We do not have remote access to these files.
* **Technical & Log Data:** Retained only for as long as necessary for the purposes outlined in this policy.

Depending on your location, you may have rights including:
* The right to access, update, or delete your personal information.
* The right to object to or restrict our processing of your data.
