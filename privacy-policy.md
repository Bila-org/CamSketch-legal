# Privacy Policy for CamSketch

**Last Updated:** March 6, 2026

**CamSketch** is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy informs you of our policies regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of personal data when you use our mobile application (the “App”).

By using the App, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

## 1.	Information Collection and Use

**CamSektch** is designed with your privacy in mind. We collect minimal data to provide and improve the service.

### a.	Information Collected:
When you use the App, we collect certain information about your device and usage:
* **Camera/Media:** The App may require access to your device’s camera to take photos or record videos. These images and videos are used only to provide App’s core functionality. They are processed locally on your device; we do not upload them to our servers unless you explicitly choose to share them.
* **Drawing & Images:** The drawings and images you create and choose to save are stored locally on your device. We do not have access to this content.
* **Notifications:** We may ask for your permission to send you notifications. If granted we use this to provide updates, alerts, or other relevant information

### b.	Device & Data Usage:
Our App uses third-party services that may automatically collect certain information about your device and usage:
* **Device Information:** Information such as your device type, operating system version, and unique device identifiers (e.g., for crash reporting).
* **Usage Data:** How the App is accessed and used (“Log Data”). This may include features you use, session length, crash reports to help us improve stability and performance.
* **Crash Data:** Technical details when the App stops unexpectedly so we can fix issues.

## 2.	Permissions
To provide App features, the App requests the following permissions:
* **Camera:** Required to take photos or record videos. The App will not function without this permission.
* **Notifications:** Used to send you updates and alerts.
* **Storage:** Used to save captured media to your device’s gallery.
  
You can change these permissions at any time through device settings.

## 3.	How We Use Your Information
We use the collected data to:
*	Provide and maintain the core camera and drawing functionality of the App.
*	Detect, prevent, and address technical issues and crashes.
*	Improve the user experience and develop new features.
*	Send you push notifications.
*	Communicate with you (if you have provided contact information).

## 4.	Third-Party Services
We rely on trusted third-party services to help us operate and improve the App. These services may collect and process data as described below
* **Google Firebase:** We use Firebase Analytics to understand usage patterns and Firebase Crashlytics to collect crash reports. Firebase may collect device identifiers and usage data. For more information, see [Google’s Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).
* **Push Notifications:** We use Firebase Cloud Messaging to deliver notifications. This service may collect a unique token for your device to enable message delivery.
  
All other features (such as loading images or storing your preferences) are handled locally on your device; no personal data is sent to external servers for these functions. 

## 5.	Data Retention and Security
* **Local Storage:** Any photos, videos, or app settings you create or adjust are stored only on your device. We do not have access to this data unless you share it with us voluntarily.
* **Cloud Storage:** Crash and analytics data is stored by Google Firebase according to its retention policies.
* **Security:** We implement reasonable security measures to protect your data. However, no system is 100% secure. We encourage you to take steps to protect your own device.

## 6.	Children’s Privacy
We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under age 13. If you are a parent or guardian and you are aware that your child has provided us with personal data, please contact us using the information in section 9.

## 8.	Changes to This Privacy Policy
We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new policy on this page and updating the “Last Updated” date. You are advised to review this policy periodically.
